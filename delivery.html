What actually happens at each step

Seven steps, start to finish. Here's what each one involves.

Step 1

Quote Request

Send us your lane, pickup and delivery dates, and freight details through the quote form, by phone, or by email. We review the load, the equipment it needs, and any special requirements, then come back with a clear, all-in rate.

Step 2

Rate Confirmation

Once you approve the rate, we lock in the load with a signed rate confirmation. Pickup and delivery details, reference numbers, and special instructions are all written down, so everyone works from the same terms.

Step 3

Carrier Matching

We match your freight with a certified, highly vetted carrier from our network, with the right equipment for the job. Operating authority, insurance, and safety record are checked before the carrier is assigned.

Step 4

Pickup & Dispatch

The driver is dispatched and checks in at the shipper on schedule. Your freight is loaded and secured, the Bill of Lading is signed, and we confirm the pickup with you.

Step 5

In-Transit Tracking

Real-time visibility from pickup to destination. If weather, traffic, or anything else changes the plan, you hear it from us first, along with what we're doing about it.

Step 6

Delivery & POD

Your freight arrives and the receiver signs the Proof of Delivery, with the delivery date and any exceptions noted. The POD comes back to us and goes straight to you.

Step 7

Invoice & Follow-Up

You receive one clean invoice with the POD attached. Then we follow up, so the next load on that lane runs just as smoothly. The relationship doesn't end at delivery.

FAQ

Common questions about delivery

How quickly can I get a rate?

Send the lane, dates, and freight details and we'll come back with a rate as quickly as we can. Specialized, oversize, and hazmat loads can take a little longer while we confirm equipment and requirements.

What equipment do you cover?

Full truckload, LTL, and partial loads, plus flatbed, step deck, Conestoga, hotshot, lowboy, RGN, heavy haul, power only, hazmat, tanker, intermodal, cross-border, and high-value freight. See Services for details.

How will I know where my load is?

We keep you updated at pickup, in transit, and at delivery by phone or email, and frequent customers can follow their loads in our customer portal.

What documents will I receive?

A rate confirmation before the load moves, the signed Bill of Lading from pickup, the signed Proof of Delivery, and an invoice with the POD attached.

What are your hours?

Our office is open Monday to Friday, 6:00 am to 8:00 pm. For a load already on the road, call 844-527-6537.
